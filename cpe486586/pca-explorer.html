Two readings of the same problem

maximize   w⊤Sw   subject to   ‖w‖ = 1
minimize   1n − 1 ∑i ‖xi − (w⊤xi)w‖2
w⊤Sw + mean squared residual = tr(S)

The two statements have the same solution, and the third line is why. Their sum is fixed, so whatever one gains the other gives up. Drag w and watch the two readouts move in opposite directions by equal amounts.

What to watch

The variance curve is a sinusoid in twice the angle. Its two peaks lie 180° apart and name the same line, since w and −w give the same projection.

At ρ = 0 with equal spreads the curve flattens. Every direction is then as good as every other, the two eigenvalues agree, and the principal direction is not defined. Set the correlation to zero and turn w to see the budget bar hold still.

Variance is not the same thing as usefulness. Nothing here consults a response variable, so the direction found is the one the predictors vary along most, which need not be the one that predicts. A feature with small spread can still carry the signal, and this method will discard it first.

The decomposition

S = 1n − 1 Xc⊤Xc,   Xc = X − 1x̄⊤
S = VΛV⊤,   V⊤V = I,   Λ = diag(λ1, λ2),   λ1 ≥ λ2
rotate: Z = XcV   gives   cov(Z) = Λ
whiten: ZΛ−1/2   gives   cov = I
λ = s11 + s222 ± √((s11 − s22)2 + 4s122)2

In two dimensions the eigenvalues have the closed form on the last line, and the first eigenvector lies at ½atan2(2s12, s11 − s22). The page uses that form here and the Jacobi method on the larger matrix in the next tab.

What to watch

Centring moves the cloud to the origin and leaves the covariance matrix untouched. The numbers do not change at all between the first two stages.

Rotating into the eigenvector basis drives the off diagonal entries to zero. The two new coordinates are uncorrelated, which is the property that earns the name principal components.

Whitening divides each new coordinate by its own standard deviation. The matrix becomes the identity and the cloud becomes round, so the direction of greatest spread is lost. Whitening is useful before some algorithms and destructive before others.

What to watch

Raise the number of true modes and the spectrum grows a longer shoulder before it drops. The position of the drop estimates the rank of the signal, and noise fills in the floor beneath it.

Push the noise up and the shoulder sinks into that floor. The rank stops being readable from the spectrum well before the signal becomes invisible in the curves themselves.

With n smaller than 24 the sample covariance matrix is singular. At most n − 1 eigenvalues can be positive, and the rest are zero whatever the data hold. Drop n below 25 and watch the tail of the spectrum vanish.

That case also inflates the share of variance kept. At n = 15 the first twelve components account for 99.9% of the total, which sounds like success and reports only how well the components fit the very sample that produced them. Every figure on this page is measured in sample, and a held out set would give smaller numbers.

Why the unit matters

x2 → cx2   sends   s22 → c2s22,   s12 → cs12
standardize:   x̃j = xj − x̄jsj,   so cov(x̃) = corr(x)

Principal components are not invariant under a change of scale on one variable. The eigenvectors of the covariance matrix turn toward whichever feature carries the larger numbers, and that feature can be made larger by writing it in a smaller unit. Running the method on the correlation matrix removes the choice, at the price of declaring every feature equally important beforehand.

Standardization is a choice rather than a repair. It declares every feature to carry equal weight, which is wrong whenever the features share a unit already and their spreads mean something, as with pixels of one image or repeated readings from one instrument. Neither matrix is the right default, and the question of which to use is a question about the measurements rather than about the method.

A third route avoids the dilemma. Where a feature has a natural reference scale, divide by that rather than by the sample standard deviation, which is itself an estimate that moves with the sample.

What to watch

In grams the mass dominates the total variance and the first component lies almost along it, with weight 1.000 on mass and 0.000 on height. In tonnes the mass is numerically tiny and the two weights trade places.

The flat line does not move at all. Standardizing first gives an answer the unit cannot touch, and it sits at 0.707 on each feature whenever the two are positively correlated.

Read the share of variance with suspicion here. At both extremes the first component accounts for almost 100% of the total, which looks like a triumph and means only that one column of numbers is far larger than the other.

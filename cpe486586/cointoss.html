The fraction of heads approaches the target as n increases. Over the same range, the difference between the head count and the tail count usually grows in absolute value.

The strip at the right of the chart is a histogram of 400 independent runs at the current n, with the normal curve predicted by theory drawn over it. Bars inside the tolerance band are red, and the rail reports how many runs they hold. That count divided by 400 estimates the probability in the weak law, which approaches one as n increases.

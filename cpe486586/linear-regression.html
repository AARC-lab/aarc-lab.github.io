The band is a simultaneous confidence band for the mean response β0 + β1x. With probability 1 − α, the true line lies inside it along its full length. The band is narrowest at x̄ and widens with distance from x̄, because an error in the slope estimate moves the line further from the truth there.

The band concerns the line, so many individual points fall outside it. A prediction band for new observations would be wider. The pointwise t band is valid for one x at a time. Along the line as a whole its coverage falls below the nominal level, which the coverage panel measures.
